# ASAMBE RURAL YOUTH EMPOWERMENT ORGANISATION (ARYEO)

**"The Youth of Good Hope for a Brighter Future"**

Official website for the **Asambe Rural Youth Empowerment Organisation (ARYEO)**, a youth-led non-profit organisation committed to empowering young people in rural communities across South Africa through education, skills development, entrepreneurship, agriculture, leadership, innovation, and community development. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## About ARYEO

Asambe Rural Youth Empowerment Organisation (ARYEO) was established on **14 January 2025** by **Thandoluhle Benya**. The organisation was founded to bridge the gap between rural and urban youth by creating opportunities that enable young people to become educated, skilled, self-reliant, and active contributors to their communities. ARYEO began its work in **eSjozini, Tugela Ferry, Msinga, KwaZulu-Natal**, with a long-term vision of expanding across South Africa. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
